# Candidate pilot invitation

Hi! I'm testing **OpenBrief**, a free, local-first Assessment Passport for people applying to internships and jobs.

It helps candidates capture what an assessment asks for, expected work effort, submission window, deadline, evaluation criteria and other terms—then keep a personal record of what happened after submission.

I'm looking for 8–10 students or recent applicants to try it for about **15–20 minutes** using a public, past or sanitised assessment brief. We'll compare manual entry with the brief importer and see where it saves time or gets things wrong.

- No payment, account or installation is needed.
- Please don't share confidential briefs, company names, personal data or your actual assessment answers.
- Nothing is uploaded automatically. The optional feedback report is exported locally and shared only if you choose.
- This is independent product research, not connected to any employer or hiring decision.

Try it here: https://openbrief-passport-im-prakashs-projects.vercel.app/

The feedback page is here: https://openbrief-passport-im-prakashs-projects.vercel.app/pilot-feedback.html

Would you be willing to test it and give honest feedback? Negative feedback is especially useful.
